# Hackathon efficiency prototype

Terms for the working prototype and its demonstration in Challenge 2.

## Language

**Working prototype**:
A runnable demonstration of a bounded solution and its efficiency evidence. Wider product features can remain future work.

**Demo path**:
The single judge-visible user journey through the working prototype, including a successful result and a relevant failure state.

**Exact fast path**:
A route that answers a supported mathematical task with a deterministic calculation before considering a general language model.

**AI fallback**:
An explicitly requested language-model answer when the exact fast path cannot satisfy a valid structured request. Its answer is unverified.

**Optional explanation**:
A student-requested description grounded in a computed answer and its actual check status.

**Symbolic check**:
An algebraic identity or root substitution check subject to stated domains and assumptions.

**Numerical evidence**:
Agreement at selected valid sample points or with numerical estimates. It does not prove a general mathematical claim.

**Unresolved result**:
A valid request for which the assistant cannot safely produce an answer within its supported scope or deadline.
